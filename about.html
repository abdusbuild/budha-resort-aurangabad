About Us

A peaceful halt on India's oldest highway, built on clean rooms, honest food and warm hospitality.

Our Story

Named for Stillness

Aurangabad sits on the Grand Trunk Road, the same road travellers have taken across North India for centuries, and an hour from Bodh Gaya, where the Buddha found enlightenment. We named our hotel for that sense of calm: a place to stop, rest and leave refreshed.

Buddha Resort Hotel & Restaurant is located at Ora–Karahra on NH-2. We welcome highway travellers, families visiting Aurangabad, business guests, pilgrims and wedding parties. We offer air-conditioned rooms, a family restaurant and space for celebrations.
