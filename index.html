A Home on the Highway

At Buddha Resort we keep things simple and sincere. Every room is air-conditioned (or fan-cooled, if you prefer), with a comfortable bed, private bathroom, flat-screen TV and polished marble floors, cleaned fresh for every guest.

Downstairs, our restaurant serves honest home-style food all day, and our banquet space hosts the region's weddings, birthdays and family functions.
