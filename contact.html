Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 12:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. If you're arriving early or leaving late, call us and we'll do our best.

Is parking available?

Yes. Free, private on-site parking is available for all guests, with CCTV coverage.

Do you have Wi-Fi?

Free Wi-Fi is available in all rooms and common areas.

Is food available at the hotel?

Yes. Our in-house family restaurant serves meals through the day, and room service is available.

Are children and pets allowed?

Children are very welcome and family rooms are available. Pets are not allowed.

How can I pay?

Please confirm accepted payment methods when you book. Some listings mention cash payment at the property.

Can you host weddings and events?

Yes. We host weddings, birthdays, family functions and corporate gatherings with in-house catering and guest rooms.
